How matching works

When you describe what you're hoping to feel or experience, we send that along with every host's listing to an AI model. It reads for meaning, not just keywords, and ranks listings by genuine fit, giving a short reason for each one.

It isn't perfect. It can misjudge tone, especially with short or ambiguous requests, and it doesn't know anything about a host beyond what they told us. Every reason is shown to you so you can judge the fit yourself, not just trust a score.
